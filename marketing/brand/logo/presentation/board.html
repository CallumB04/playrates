What we heard

The brief

A video game tracker where players log, rate and review what they play, manage a backlog and wishlist, and talk with friends and the community. The mark should feel confident and opinionated, with a bit of play, and hold up as a 16px tab icon as well as a phone home screen.

confidentopinionatedplayfulmodern

Success: stands apart from gamepads, pixel art and trophies; recognisable at 16 px; works in one colour; carries the existing iris and ember palette.

Final design

Verdict

The full stop is the verdict: the square knocked out of the P drops to the baseline as the last word on a game.

  • Keeps the ember full stop the old PR. favicon already had
  • The counter and the stop are the same square, so the mark is built from one unit
  • A pixel-snapped cut keeps it crisp at 16 and 32 px
